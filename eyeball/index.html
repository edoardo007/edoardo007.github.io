How good is your built-in least-squares solver?

Each round shows a random scatter plot. Estimate one property of its least-squares line by eye, type it in, and see how close you got. Three rounds per game, up to 100 points per estimate.

Each round, the first point lands at the smallest x plus up to 5 % of the width. Noise is the standard deviation of the scatter, as a percentage of how much the line rises or falls over the x range. 0 % puts every point on the line.

How is it scored?
  • The answer is the ordinary least-squares line through the points shown, not the hidden line they were generated from.
  • Slope is scored by the angle the line makes on screen: full marks if you match it, zero at 15° off. Steep axes or huge numbers are no harder than small ones.
  • y-intercept: zero points once you are off by 10 % of the plotted y range. On Easy, Medium and Hard the data start away from x = 0, so you have to extrapolate: further on harder levels.
  • R² is the fraction of the variance in y explained by the line (1 = all points on it). Zero points once you are 0.2 off.
